# Narratai - Requirements (Rewards + Progress)

## Reward Tiers
1. Finish a chapter → sticker reward
2. Finish all chapters in a story → badge reward
3. Finish the whole book → trophy reward

## Progress Tracking
- Show a progress bar for completion
- Progress should be shown per chapter (paragraphs) and per book (chapters)
- Placement: reading screen and chapter end screen

## Reward Format
- Rewards should be visual (stickers/images), not just text

## Micro-Achievements
- First recording
- First chapter completed
- Three chapters completed
- Story completed
